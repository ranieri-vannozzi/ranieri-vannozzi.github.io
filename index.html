    Overview

I am a tech lead and software architect with 18 years of experience in enterprise software, 8 of them leading engineering teams. I started in QA in 2008 and grew through full stack development into team lead, head of software development and head of IT platform product development roles, working for finance, banking and industrial clients such as Intesa Sanpaolo, Sisal, SIA and Cerved. I get productive quickly on existing codebases, shape the architecture and the delivery process together with the team, and stay hands-on in the code, from Java, Kotlin and Node.js backends to Angular frontends and Android apps. Since 2025 I have been working as a freelancer under the HeroHubs brand, consulting enterprise clients on cloud-native backends on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, with AI as part of my daily workflow.
